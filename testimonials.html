Student Impact

Voices of Transformation

Behind every aggregated number is an individual human story of resilience, opportunity, and triumph over circumstance.

Scholarship & Mentorship Hyderabad

From Daily Wage Uncertainty to Engineering College

When Rehan scored 94% in intermediate exams, his father, an auto-rickshaw driver, worried about college counseling fees. Through Chamak Foundation's merit scholarship and senior mentorship, Rehan secured admission to a top engineering institution.

“Chamak gave me the confidence that poverty would not write the final chapter of my career.”

— Mohd. Rehan, 19
Girl Child Education Charminar Area

Defying the Drop-out Odds in Class 9

After losing her mother, Fatima was on the verge of being withdrawn from school. Chamak volunteers intervened with targeted family counseling, sponsored tuition fees, and paired her with a woman mentor. Today, she is top of her class.

“My teachers told me I had wings; Chamak made sure I had the sky to fly in.”

— Fatima Begum, 15
Community Libraries Rural Telangana

A Neighbourhood Transformed by Books

Before Chamak established quiet reading rooms, local students had no study tables or books in their homes. Today, the library serves over 60 children daily with curriculum texts, storybooks, and competitive exam guides.

“The library is our second home. We study together, read novels, and dream bigger.”

— Syed Ayan & Friends
