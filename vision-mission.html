Why We Exist

Vision. Mission. Purpose.

What Chamak Foundation believes in, and the change we're working toward.

Our Vision

A society where every child has access to education and the opportunity to achieve their dreams.

Our Mission

To empower underprivileged children through quality education, scholarships and guidance.

Our Objectives

What we work toward, every day

01

Quality education for all

Promote quality education for all children, regardless of background.

02

Scholarships

Provide financial support through scholarships for meritorious and needy students.

03

Career guidance

Guide students for a better career future with mentoring and planning.

04

Life skills & confidence

Develop life skills and build confidence in every child we support.

05

Girl child education

Empower girls through education, removing barriers that hold them back.

06

Community libraries

Build community libraries in underserved areas to keep learning alive.

“Education is not simply preparation for life. It can be the beginning of a different life.”

— A principle Chamak Foundation is built on
