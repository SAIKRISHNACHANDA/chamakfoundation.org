Chamak Foundation (Chamak Padhai Se)

Chamak Foundation (Chamak Padhai Se) is a non-governmental organization committed to empowering students through education and creating opportunities for young people to build a brighter and more dignified future.

The Foundation was launched on 6 October 2023 at the residence of Professor Shanta Sinha in Hyderabad, in the presence of Professor Shanta Sinha, renowned educationist and child-rights activist, and R. Venkat Reddy, National Convenor, MV Foundation. The Foundation's Founder President, Dr. Mohammad Azam Ali, was also present on the occasion.

The establishment of Chamak Foundation was inspired by the belief that education is the most powerful instrument for transforming lives and creating an equitable society. The organization seeks to support students, particularly those facing educational and socio-economic challenges, by providing guidance, mentoring, learning resources, career counselling, and opportunities for personal and academic development.

Core Objective

“Empower Students Through Education.”

Chamak Foundation believes that every student deserves access to quality education, proper guidance, encouragement, and opportunities to realize their potential. Through its educational and community initiatives, the Foundation strives to help students learn, grow, dream, and achieve.

Vision

To create an educated, empowered and inclusive society where every student has the opportunity to learn, succeed and contribute meaningfully to the nation.

Mission

Chamak Foundation works towards:

- Promoting access to education and learning opportunities

- Providing academic and career guidance to students

- Supporting students from economically disadvantaged backgrounds

- Encouraging language and communication skills

- Developing life skills, confidence and leadership qualities

- Promoting reading habits and access to educational resources

- Supporting the eradication of child labour through education

- Motivating young people to become responsible and active citizens

- Creating awareness about the importance of education, skills and lifelong learning

Our Belief

Education does not merely prepare a student for a career; it gives them the confidence, knowledge and power to transform their life and society.

Padmanagar · 14 Nov 2025 Career Guidance Programme “Build a Positive Attitude, Believe in Yourself, Read Every Day, and Prepare for a Brighter Future.” +

Career Guidance Programme

Chamak Foundation (Chamak Padhai Se) organized a Career Guidance Programme at Government High School, Padmanagar, on 14 November 2025.

The programme aimed to guide and motivate students to develop a positive attitude towards education, self-confidence, and effective reading habits. Students were encouraged to understand the importance of setting clear goals, identifying their strengths, and working consistently towards their future aspirations.

During the programme, students were sensitized to the importance of self-confidence and a positive mindset in achieving academic and personal success. They were also encouraged to cultivate regular reading habits, expand their knowledge beyond textbooks, and make effective use of books and other learning resources.

The programme emphasized that career success begins with the right attitude, continuous learning, confidence and disciplined effort. Students were motivated to dream big, believe in themselves, and prepare themselves for future educational and career opportunities.

Key Message

“Build a Positive Attitude, Believe in Yourself, Read Every Day, and Prepare for a Brighter Future.”

Through such career guidance and motivational initiatives, Chamak Foundation (Chamak Padhai Se) continues its mission of empowering students through education and helping young learners discover their potential.

Rangpoor Village · 5 Nov 2024 Digital Detox Awareness “Reduce Screen Time. Read More Books. Learn More. Live Better.” +

Digital Detox Awareness Programme

Chamak Foundation (Chamak Padhai Se), in collaboration with Hill Top Education Center, Huzurabad, organized a Digital Detox Awareness Programme at Rangpoor Village on 5 November 2024.

The programme was conducted to sensitize students about the excessive use of mobile phones, social media and digital screens and to encourage them to develop healthier and more productive habits.

Students were motivated to reduce their screen time, avoid unnecessary dependence on digital devices, and cultivate the habit of reading books. The programme highlighted the importance of balancing technology with reading, learning, physical activities, creativity and meaningful social interaction.

The initiative aimed to help students understand that technology is a useful tool when used wisely, but excessive screen time can distract them from their studies, reading habits and overall development.

Key Message

“Reduce Screen Time. Read More Books. Learn More. Live Better.”

Through the Digital Detox initiative, Chamak Foundation reaffirmed its commitment to empowering students through education and promoting healthy learning habits among young people.

Free English Grammar Books Distribution Programme

Chamak Foundation (Chamak Padhai Se) organized a Free English Grammar Books Distribution Programme at ZPHS, Nallabelli, Warangal, on 16 December 2024.

The programme also emphasized the importance of language development as an essential part of academic and personal growth. Students were encouraged to make effective use of the books, read regularly, practice English, and continue their pursuit of knowledge.

Key Message

“A Book Can Open a Door to a Brighter Future.”

This initiative reflects the vision of Chamak Foundation — Chamak Padhai Se, to make education more accessible, meaningful and empowering for every student.

Huzurabad · 11 Jul 2026 Life Skills for 21st-Century A Life Skills Development Programme on “Life Skills for 21st-Century Challenges” was conducted on 11 July 2026 at the Mahatma Jyotiba Phule Telangana Backward Classes Welfare Residential Educational Institutions Society School, Huzurabad. +

Life Skills for 21st-Century Challenges

A Life Skills Development Programme on “Life Skills for 21st-Century Challenges” was conducted on 11 July 2026 at the Mahatma Jyotiba Phule Telangana Backward Classes Welfare Residential Educational Institutions Society School, Huzurabad.

Dr. Mohammad Azam Ali, Assistant Professor of English and motivational speaker, was the resource person for the programme. He interacted with the students and highlighted the importance of developing essential life skills to face the challenges of the modern world.

Dr. Azam Ali encouraged the students to cultivate creativity, effective communication, collaboration, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills. He explained that academic knowledge alone is not sufficient to succeed in life and that students must develop the confidence and skills required to deal with real-life situations and challenges.

He motivated the students to think creatively, work effectively with others, communicate confidently, analyse problems from different perspectives, and find practical solutions. He emphasized that these 21st-century skills would help students become confident, responsible, adaptable, and successful individuals.

The programme provided the students with valuable insights into personal development, employability, leadership, and preparing themselves to face the realities and challenges of life.

Hasanparthi, Warangal · 14 Feb 2026 SSC Examination Orientation “Examinations are a part of life, but marks do not determine your life. Believe in yourself, develop your skills and keep moving forward.” +

Telangana SSC Examination Orientation Programme

Chamak Foundation (Chamak Padhai Se) organized an Orientation Programme on Telangana SSC Examinations at ZPHS Girls High School, Hasanparthi, Warangal, on 14 February 2026.

The programme was conducted to motivate and guide students appearing for the SSC examinations and to help them approach their exams with confidence, a positive mindset and effective preparation skills.

Dr. Tamme Anuradha was the speaker of the programme. She motivated the students to develop self-confidence, study skills, a positive attitude and the ability to face examinations with courage and determination.

Dr. Anuradha emphasized that examinations are only a part of life and marks alone do not determine a person's future or worth. She encouraged students to prepare sincerely, give their best, believe in their abilities and continue developing their skills irrespective of their examination results.

The students were encouraged to overcome examination fear, remain focused on their goals and understand that success in life depends not only on marks but also on knowledge, skills, confidence, character and perseverance.

Key Message

“Examinations are a part of life, but marks do not determine your life. Believe in yourself, develop your skills and keep moving forward.”

The programme reflected Chamak Foundation’s commitment to empowering students through education, motivation and confidence-building, enabling young learners to face academic challenges with courage and a positive outlook.

Eco-Friendly Clay Ganesha Idols campaign

The NSS Unit of Vaagdevi Engineering College, Warangal, organized a remarkable initiative to promote environmental conservation and create awareness about the harmful effects of Plaster of Paris (PoP) idols during the Ganesh Chaturthi festival. The event focused on encouraging people to use eco-friendly clay Ganesh idols to prevent water pollution and preserve aquatic ecosystems.The program saw enthusiastic participation from NSS volunteers, who actively engaged in crafting clay idols on campus. Under the guidance of faculty members, the volunteers showcased their creativity and dedication to the cause. The clay idols were later distributed to the local community near the college premises, fostering a spirit of environmental responsibility among the residents.

The event was well-received by the local community, with many residents appreciating the college's efforts to promote eco-consciousness. This activity not only reinforced the NSS volunteers' commitment to social and environmental causes but also served as a platform to educate people on the significance of adopting eco-friendly practices.

Through this initiative, the NSS Unit of Vaagdevi Engineering College reaffirmed its dedication to making a positive impact on society and nurturing environmentally responsible citizens.

Swachhata Hi Seva Cleanliness Campaign

“Swachhta Hi Seva” Campaign Organized by NSS Unit of Vaagdevi Engineering College, Warangal

The NSS Unit of Vaagdevi Engineering College, Warangal, successfully organized the “Swachhta Hi Seva” campaign to promote cleanliness and raise awareness among people about the importance of maintaining a clean and hygienic environment. This initiative is part of the nationwide program launched by the Hon’ble Prime Minister of India, Shri Narendra Modi, aimed at fostering a culture of cleanliness and civic responsibility.The event witnessed enthusiastic participation from NSS volunteers, who actively engaged in various activities to support the cause. The program began with an inaugural session, where the importance of cleanliness and its impact on public health, environmental sustainability, and national progress was highlighted.

Key Activities Undertaken During the Program:

Cleanliness Drives: Volunteers conducted cleanliness drives in and around the college premises, nearby villages, and public spaces, ensuring the removal of waste and debris.

Awareness Campaigns: Volunteers interacted with residents, spreading awareness about the significance of proper waste management, segregation of biodegradable and non-biodegradable waste, and the harmful effects of plastic pollution.

Tree Plantation: As part of the event, saplings were planted to promote greenery and environmental conservation. Pledge for Cleanliness: Participants took a pledge to uphold the values of cleanliness and hygiene in their daily lives and encourage others to do the same.

The program not only emphasized the importance of cleanliness but also inspired volunteers and the community to take collective responsibility for creating a cleaner and healthier environment.

The NSS Unit of Vaagdevi Engineering College remains committed to organizing such initiatives that contribute to the betterment of society and align with the vision of a “Swachh Bharat.” The Principal of Vaagdevi Engineering College appreciated the efforts of the NSS volunteers and reiterated the institution’s dedication to promoting social welfare activities. The event concluded with a vote of thanks to all participants and contributors for their active involvement in making the campaign a success.

“Cleanliness is not a one-time activity; it is a continuous process for a better tomorrow.”

Orientation Programme for Newly joined Volunteers

Report on NSS Orientation Programme for Newly Enrolled Volunteers at Vaagdevi Engineering College The National Service Scheme (NSS) unit of Vaagdevi Engineering College successfully organized an

Orientation Programme for newly enrolled NSS volunteers. The event aimed to introduce the core objectives, vision, and mission of NSS while highlighting its transformative impact on students' lives and society.

The session began with an engaging introduction to the values and principles of NSS, emphasizing the motto, “Not Me, But You,” which underscores the essence of selfless service and community welfare. Faculty coordinators explained how participation in NSS activities fosters qualities such as leadership, teamwork, social responsibility, and ethical values, equipping volunteers to become better individuals and contributors to societal development.

The enthusiasm of the students was evident, with over 100 volunteers eagerly enrolling in the NSS unit. The program also included an overview of the upcoming activities and initiatives, such as plantation drives, health camps, cleanliness campaigns, and skill development workshops.

The newly joined volunteers expressed their excitement and commitment to contributing to meaningful causes under the NSS banner. The orientation concluded with a motivational address by the program officer, encouraging students to actively participate in the journey of creating a better and sustainable society.

The event marked a promising beginning for the new NSS batch, setting the stage for impactful community service and personal growth.
