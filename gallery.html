How to update events every day:

Our gallery is engineered with a modular, scalable architecture. Whenever an event happens:

  1. Place your event photographs into public/assets/img/.
  2. Open src/data/galleryEvents.js (or edit gallery.html) and append a new event object with title, date, and photo URLs.
  3. Choose your card grid style: featured-mosaic (1 hero + 2 stacked), split-duo (2 equal), or trio-strip (3 action tiles).

Have field photos or daily reports?

Send high-resolution photos and brief event notes directly to the Chamak Foundation media team for instant publication.
